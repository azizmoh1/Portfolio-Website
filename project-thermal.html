Thermal-Fluids Experimental Research

Research Assistant · Intelligent Multiphase Systems Lab · UIC · March 2026–Present

The engineering problem

I help develop an experimental platform for studying how boiling coolant removes heat from high-performance electronics. My role connects the mechanical hardware with the sensors and wiring needed to run repeatable experiments.

Design, prototype and refine

I model components in SolidWorks and use FDM and SLA printing to check fit, fluid routing and sensor access. I consider how the parts will be made and assembled while refining the design, including prototypes printed in Rigid 10K resin.

Connect the hardware to useful measurements

I support flow measurement, sensors and data-acquisition hardware—the equipment that records test signals. My work includes signal wiring, power connections, calibration support and troubleshooting, bringing the mechanical and measurement systems together.

Progress and engineering experience

The platform is in development. My contributions center on hardware prototypes and instrumentation integration. This work is building my ability to design around both physical constraints and the needs of an experiment.
