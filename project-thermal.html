Thermal-Fluids Experimental Research

Research Assistant · Intelligent Multiphase Systems Lab · UIC · March 2026–Present

Research Focus

I am contributing to the development of a precision flow-boiling test platform to study multiphase heat transfer and interfacial transport for high-performance electronics cooling.

Experimental Hardware

I design and prototype hardware in SolidWorks, accounting for fluid routing, mechanical interfaces, instrumentation access, and manufacturability. I use FDM and SLA printing, including Rigid 10K resin, to prototype components and iterate on fit, fluid routing and instrumentation access.

Instrumentation and Integration

My work includes flow measurement, DAQ hardware, sensors, signal wiring, and power connections, with support for calibration, troubleshooting, validation, and repeatable data acquisition.

Current Status

The platform is under development. This page describes my hardware and integration contributions; completed research findings will be added as the experimental work progresses.
